

## Professional Agency Website Header

### Overview
A sleek, corporate-style header with transparent-to-solid scroll behavior, centered navigation with Bangla menu items, and auth buttons with modal dialogs.

---

### 1. Header Component
- **Initial State**: Fully transparent background over hero section
- **On Scroll**: Smoothly transitions to solid dark green (#0f3d2e) with subtle bottom shadow
- **Transition**: 0.3s ease-in-out for smooth effect
- **Fixed position**: Stays at top while scrolling

---

### 2. Layout Structure
| Left | Center | Right |
|------|--------|-------|
| Logo (image placeholder) | Navigation Menu | Login + Sign Up buttons |

---

### 3. Navigation Menu (Bangla)
Centered menu items with hover effects:
- Facebook Ads
- Web Development  
- Graphics Design
- Video Editing
- Motion Graphics
- About Us
- Contact Us

**Hover behavior**: Color changes to red with subtle underline animation

---

### 4. Auth Buttons
**Login Button:**
- Transparent background, white text, green border
- Hover: Background fills dark green, white text remains
- Opens login modal dialog

**Sign Up Button:**
- Solid red (#c4161c), white text
- Hover: Slightly darker red, subtle scale (1.03x)
- Opens signup modal dialog

---

### 5. Mobile Experience
- Hamburger menu icon appears on smaller screens
- Opens slide-in drawer from right side
- Contains all navigation items + auth buttons
- Same color scheme and hover effects

---

### 6. Hero Section
A simple hero section below the header to demonstrate the transparent-to-solid scroll transition effect

---

### Design Notes
- Clean, serious corporate aesthetic
- No gradients or glassmorphism
- Professional color palette (dark green, red, white)
- Trustworthy political-style authority adapted for business
